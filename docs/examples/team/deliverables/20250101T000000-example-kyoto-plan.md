foodie -> planner: Kyoto food & culture, 3 days (~12 lines):

DAY 1 — Southern Higashiyama & Gion
• Kiyomizu-dera — hilltop temple with the huge wooden veranda and city panorama.
• Sannenzaka & Ninenzaka — preserved stone slopes; the classic Kyoto streetscape.
• Yasaka Shrine + Gion — lantern-lined shrine spilling into the geisha district at dusk.
• Eat: Kagizen Yoshifusa (Gion) for warabimochi; Nishiki Market downtown for tako-tamago (octopus-stuffed egg) and tsukemono pickles.
• Tip: Don't eat while walking in Gion, and never photograph geiko/maiko on private lanes (posted, with fines).

DAY 2 — Fushimi & Arashiyama
• Fushimi Inari Taisha — thousands of vermilion torii tunnels up the mountain (arrive before 8am).
• Tenryu-ji + Arashiyama Bamboo Grove — UNESCO Zen garden paired with the famous grove.
• Eat: Inari-zushi at the Fushimi Inari food stalls (the fox's favorite); yudofu (tofu hotpot) at Shoraian in Arashiyama.
• Tip: Shrine manners = bow twice, clap twice, bow once; at Buddhist temples, don't clap — just bow.

DAY 3 — Northwest & Downtown
• Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) — gold-leaf Zen temple mirrored over its pond.
• Ryoan-ji — the famous dry rock garden; sit and count the 15 stones (only 14 visible at once).
• Eat: Honke Owariya (downtown, soba since 1465) for soba; Nishiki Market for dashimaki tamago and Aritsugu knives.
• Tip: At Nishiki Market, don't eat while walking (tabearuki) — stand beside the stall; many shops are cash-only.

Note: Day 3 pairs best if you start northwest (Kinkaku-ji/Ryoan-ji) then ride the bus/train downtown to Nishiki. Happy to expand any day.

GETTING IN / MODES
- From Kyoto Station: Karasuma Subway Line (station under the station, K11) goes straight north into downtown; the huge bus terminal at the Karasuma exit (Kyoto Eki-mae) covers most sights; JR lines (Nara/Sagano) leave from the same complex.
- Main modes: City Bus (flat ~¥230, best coverage but traffic-prone), Subway (Karasuma N-S, Tozai E-W, ~¥220-360), and private rail (Keihan, Hankyu, JR, Keifuku) for cross-town hops.
- Ticket advice: buy an ICOCA (JR West) at Kyoto Station — works on bus/subway/JR/private rail and in shops; Suica also works. For 3 days, load ICOCA and add a pass only on heavy days: Subway 1-Day ¥800, Subway & Bus 1-Day ¥1100, Subway & Bus 2-Day ¥2000. (Bus-only 1-day pass was discontinued — don't count on it.)

PER DAY (geographic clusters)
- Day 1 – South/East Higashiyama: Fushimi Inari (JR Nara Line, 2 stops ~5 min, or Keihan to Fushimi-Inari) → Tofuku-ji → Kiyomizu-dera/Gion. Connect with Keihan Line (Fushimi-Inari → Gion-Shijo) + bus 206; ~20-30 min between clusters.
- Day 2 – Arashiyama + NW: JR Sagano Line Kyoto→Saga-Arashiyama ~15 min; then Randen tram or bus 28/59 to Kinkaku-ji/Ryoan-ji. Note this cluster is far west — do it as one block, ~30-45 min between Arashiyama and Kinkaku-ji.
- Day 3 – Central/North Higashiyama: Nijo Castle → Nishiki Market → Ginkaku-ji/Philosopher's Path/Nanzen-ji. Use Tozai Subway Line (Nijojo-mae → Higashiyama) then bus 5/100; ~15-25 min hops.

ARRIVAL / DEPARTURE TIPS
- ICOCA from JR ticket office; Kansai Airport to Kyoto via JR Haruka (~75 min) or limousine bus.
- Luggage forwarding (takuhaibin) from hotel front desk or convenience store, ~¥2,000/bag next-day — great for a Kyoto→Osaka/Tokyo leg.
- Base a hotel around Kyoto Station (best transit hub) or Shijo-Karasuma/Kawaramachi (downtown, walkable, subway+bus).

GOTCHA
- Bus 206/100 and Higashiyama routes are jam-packed 8-9am and 4-6pm, and buses crawl in traffic — prefer subway for N-S/E-W runs, or walk Kiyomizu→Gion. Also: most temples close 16:30-17:00 (some 18:00 seasonal), and many small shops/eateries are cash-only.
